Questions, answered

Anything else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp — we reply fast.

Does teeth whitening damage the enamel?

No. Professional in-clinic whitening uses enamel-safe gels applied and monitored by a dentist. Unlike over-the-finish-line home kits, the strength is matched to your teeth, and we check for cavities or sensitivity first so the treatment stays safe and comfortable.

How long does a dental implant take from start to finish?

Most cases take 2–4 months in total: the implant is placed in a short procedure, allowed to fuse with the jawbone, and then fitted with your permanent crown. In many cases you leave the first appointment with a temporary tooth, so you're never without a smile.

Do you offer instalment plans?

Yes. Major treatments such as implants, aligners and full-mouth rehabilitation can be split into easy monthly instalments at 0% markup. We'll agree the full schedule in writing before treatment begins — no surprises later.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Message us on WhatsApp or call the clinic right away — we hold same-day emergency slots for severe pain, broken teeth and lost crowns. If a tooth is knocked out, hold it by the crown (not the root), keep it in milk or inside your cheek, and reach us within the hour for the best chance of saving it.
