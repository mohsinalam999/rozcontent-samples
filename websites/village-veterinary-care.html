Why it matters

Most pet problems are small — right up until they aren't

The difference between a routine visit and an emergency is usually time. The owners who fare best aren't the ones with the healthiest pets — they're the ones with a vet who already knows their pet.

01

The bad breath that isn't normal

Dental disease hides in plain sight — often the only sign is the smell owners have learned to live with. A dental check catches it while it's still a clean, not a surgery.

02

The lump you'll "watch"

Early is everything with lumps, limps and appetite changes. In-house imaging and pathology mean answers start here, not after a referral and a week's worry.

03

The kitten or puppy decision

Desexing and vaccination timing shape a pet's whole health story. Getting the sequence right from the first visit is the cheapest insurance there is.
