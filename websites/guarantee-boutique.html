The 11pm problem

Thousands of likes. One pair of hands answering chats.

Every Guarantee Boutique post pulls in an audience most shops would envy. But there is no shop behind the posts — so every single admirer has to open a chat and start from zero: “price?” … “size?” … “available?”

Meanwhile the boutique answers each one by hand, one at a time — and the customer who fell in love with a dress at midnight has usually scrolled on by morning. The likes were never the problem. The missing shop is.

  • ✕Orders bottlenecked through one chat thread, answered manually, in order, whenever she’s awake.
  • ✕Yesterday’s viral post is buried by tomorrow — the pieces in it stop selling even while they’re still in stock.

Good questions

Before you tap order

How do I pay?

Payment is arranged directly with the boutique in your WhatsApp chat when you confirm the order — the same conversation where you confirm your size and delivery address.

Why does ordering happen on WhatsApp?

Because that’s how Guarantee Boutique has always sold — WhatsApp 0308 7874678 is the boutique’s only official order channel. This catalogue simply removes the back-and-forth in front of it: prices and sizes are here, and your order arrives in the chat already written.

What if my size isn’t listed?

Sizes on each card show the usual range. Tap the order button anyway and ask in the chat — the boutique confirms exact availability before anything is final.

I saw a piece on TikTok that isn’t here. Can I still get it?

Yes — message the boutique on WhatsApp and mention the post. If the piece is still in stock, it can be added to the catalogue and ordered the same way.
