## What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that is placed into the jawbone, where it acts as an artificial tooth root. Over a period of weeks, the bone naturally fuses with the implant in a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation. Once healed, a connector (abutment) and a custom-made crown are attached on top, giving you a tooth that looks, feels, and functions much like a natural one.

Unlike a denture, an implant does not move, slip, or need to be removed at night. Unlike a bridge, it does not require grinding down the healthy teeth on either side. A single implant replaces a single tooth; multiple implants can also support a bridge or stabilise a full denture for patients missing many teeth.

## Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults with one or more missing teeth can be considered for implants, but a proper assessment matters. Generally, good candidates have:

- **Healthy gums** and no untreated gum disease
- **Sufficient jawbone** to support the implant (or willingness to consider bone grafting if bone volume is low)
- **Good general health**, including well-managed conditions such as diabetes
- **A commitment to oral hygiene** and regular dental follow-ups

Heavy smoking can slow healing and reduce long-term success, so your dentist will discuss this honestly with you. Age alone is rarely a barrier — implants are routinely placed for patients in their 60s and 70s — but implants are not placed in teenagers whose jaws are still growing. The only way to know for certain is an in-person examination with X-rays or a 3D CBCT scan, which is why any reputable dentist in DHA Lahore will assess you before quoting a final treatment plan.

## The Dental Implant Process, Step by Step

**Step 1: Consultation and planning.** Your dentist examines your mouth, reviews your medical history, and takes imaging to measure bone quality and plan the exact implant position.

**Step 2: Preparatory treatment (if needed).** A failing tooth may need extraction first, and some patients require a bone graft or sinus lift to create enough support. This adds healing time but greatly improves the odds of a strong result.

**Step 3: Implant placement.** Under local anaesthesia, the titanium implant is placed into the jawbone in a minor surgical procedure, usually completed in under an hour per implant. Most patients manage any post-procedure discomfort with standard pain relief and return to desk work within a day or two.

**Step 4: Healing and integration.** Over the next 2–4 months, the implant fuses with the bone. You may wear a temporary tooth during this period, so you are never left with a visible gap.

**Step 5: Crown placement.** Once integration is confirmed, impressions or digital scans are taken, and your permanent crown is fabricated and fitted. The treatment is complete — you leave with a tooth that is fixed, functional, and matched to your natural teeth.

## How Long Does the Whole Process Take?

From first consultation to final crown, most single-implant cases in Lahore take **three to six months**, most of which is healing time rather than time in the chair. Cases requiring bone grafting may take longer, because the graft needs its own healing period before the implant is placed. The actual appointments are few: consultation, placement, a review visit, and crown fitting. Your dentist will give you a personalised timeline at your first visit, since healing rates differ from person to person.

## Dental Implants vs Bridges and Dentures

- **Longevity:** With proper care, implants can last 20 years or more. Bridges typically need replacement every 10–15 years, and dentures often need relining or replacement every 5–8 years.
- **Effect on other teeth:** A bridge requires reshaping the neighbouring healthy teeth. Implants leave adjacent teeth untouched.
- **Bone health:** Implants stimulate the jawbone like natural roots, helping prevent the bone shrinkage that gradually changes facial shape after tooth loss. Dentures and bridges do not.
- **Comfort and function:** Implants are fixed and allow near-normal chewing. Removable dentures can slip and limit certain foods.
- **Upfront cost:** Dentures are cheapest initially, bridges sit in the middle, and implants cost the most upfront — but often prove economical over a lifetime because they rarely need replacement.

## Aftercare: Making Your Implant Last

An implant cannot get a cavity, but the gum and bone around it can still become infected if neglected. Long-term success comes down to simple habits: brush twice daily, clean between teeth (floss or interdental brushes around the implant crown), avoid using your teeth as tools, and attend check-ups every six months so your dentist can monitor the implant early if any issue develops. If you grind your teeth at night, ask about a night guard — it protects both implants and natural teeth.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does getting a dental implant hurt?**
The procedure is done under local anaesthesia, so you should not feel pain during placement. Mild soreness and swelling for a few days afterwards is normal and usually managed with prescribed painkillers and a soft diet.

**How long do dental implants last?**
With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, implants are designed to last many years — clinical studies report survival rates above 90–95% over 10–15 years. No dentist can guarantee a specific lifespan, but proper placement and aftercare give implants the best longevity of any tooth-replacement option.

**Can I get an implant years after losing a tooth?**
Usually, yes. However, bone shrinks gradually where a tooth is missing, so a longer gap may mean bone grafting is needed first. A CBCT scan will show exactly what is possible in your case.

**Are dental implants safe for diabetics?**
Patients with well-controlled diabetes are often good candidates, as healing can proceed normally. Uncontrolled diabetes raises the risk of slow healing and infection, so your dentist will review your recent HbA1c and may coordinate with your physician.

**How many visits will I need?**
Most patients need four to five visits in total: consultation and scans, implant placement, a post-operative review, and one or two appointments for the crown. Pearl Dental Studio plans these around your schedule wherever possible.
